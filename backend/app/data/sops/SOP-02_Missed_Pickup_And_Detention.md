# Standard Operating Procedure: SOP-02 - Missed Pickup and Facility Detention

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Department:** Carrier Operations & Billing  
**Audience:** Dispatchers, Carrier Procurement, Logistics Coordinators  

---

## 1. Missed Pickup Escalation
A missed pickup occurs when a dispatched carrier fails to arrive at the shipper facility within **30 minutes** after the scheduled pickup appointment window without providing advance notice.

### Actions Required:
1. **Direct Communication:** Call driver and carrier dispatch immediately. Confirm status and current GPS position.
2. **Grace Period:** A maximum grace period of **45 minutes** is permitted if the carrier provides proof of heavy traffic or unavoidable facility hold-up.
3. **Load Recovery / Repower:**
   - If carrier is unresponsive after 30 minutes, or ETA exceeds 2 hours past window, unassign carrier immediately.
   - Status updated to `pending` in the load board.
   - Reassign load to an approved backup carrier on the designated lane.
   - Assess non-performance fee ($150) against the offending carrier's record.

## 2. Shipper and Receiver Detention Billing Rules
Detention occurs when a driver is held at a shipping or receiving facility beyond the standard free-time allowance.

### Standard Detention Policy:
- **Free Time Allowance:** **2 hours** standard loading/unloading free time starting from driver's confirmed facility check-in timestamp.
- **Detention Rate:** **$50.00 per hour** after the initial 2 free hours, billed in 15-minute increments.
- **Documentation Requirements:**
  1. Signed bill of lading (BOL) with clear in and out timestamps marked by security or dock manager.
  2. Electronic geofence check-in timestamp via mobile app or ELD.
  3. Notice must be sent to the broker/customer within 30 minutes of entering detention (at hour 2.0). Failure to notify shipper prior to departure voids detention claims.

## 3. Layover Policy
If shipper cannot load the truck on the scheduled pickup date, carrier is entitled to a **$250.00 layover fee** provided driver remained on site ready to load.
