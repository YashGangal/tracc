# Standard Operating Procedure: SOP-03 - Refrigerated Cargo Temperature Excursion & Claims

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Department:** Claims, Quality & Refrigerated Fleet Operations  
**Audience:** Reefer Dispatchers, Safety Officers, Claims Specialists  

---

## 1. Temperature Control Obligations
Carriers hauling temperature-controlled commodities (Fresh Produce, Dairy, Pharmaceuticals, Frozen Meat) must maintain continuous pre-trip inspection documentation and continuous electronic reefer downloads.

### Standard Setpoint Guidelines:
- **Frozen Freight (Ice Cream, Poultry, Frozen Seafood):** -10°F to -0°F (Continuous Run mode required).
- **Fresh Produce (Berries, Leafy Greens):** +34°F to +38°F (Cycle Sentry permitted only if shipper specified).
- **Pharmaceuticals:** Strict ambient (+59°F to +77°F) or cold-chain (+36°F to +46°F).

## 2. Temperature Excursion Management
An excursion occurs when the trailer interior temperature deviates by more than **+/- 4°F** from bill of lading specified setpoint for a duration greater than **45 minutes**.

### Immediate Actions:
1. **Driver Command:** Direct driver to verify reefer display setpoint, fuel level, and check for fault diagnostic error codes (e.g., Code 18: High Discharge Pressure, Code 20: Engine Failed to Start).
2. **Reefer Repair Assistance:** Route vehicle to nearest Thermo King or Carrier Transicold authorized dealer immediately.
3. **Pulping Cargo:** At destination, receiver has legal right to pulp core temperatures using certified temperature probes. If product exceeds safe threshold:
   - Request formal joint inspection.
   - Retain reefer data logger continuous printout / telematics log.
   - Do not allow driver to depart dock until cargo disposition (acceptance, rework, rejection) is confirmed in writing on the BOL.

## 3. OS&D Claim Reporting
All cargo claims must be filed within **30 days** of delivery. Carrier liability is governed by the Carmack Amendment.
