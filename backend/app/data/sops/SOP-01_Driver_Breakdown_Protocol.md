# Standard Operating Procedure: SOP-01 - Tractor-Trailer Breakdown Protocol

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Department:** Dispatch & Fleet Operations  
**Audience:** All Freight Dispatchers, Fleet Managers, and Operations Leads  

---

## 1. Purpose
This SOP defines the mandatory operational procedures to be executed when a driver or carrier experiences an equipment breakdown, mechanical failure, or roadside emergency while in transit on an active load.

## 2. Immediate Initial Actions (Within 15 Minutes)
1. **Driver Safety Verification:** Confirm the driver is in a safe location (e.g., highway shoulder with emergency triangles deployed, weigh station, or rest area).
2. **Obtain Critical Incident Details:**
   - Exact Mile Marker, Interstate/Highway name, and nearest town/exit.
   - Load Number and commodity type (especially if Reefer or hazardous cargo).
   - Nature of breakdown: engine failure, blowout, brake line rupture, transmission failure, reefer unit code.
   - ETA estimate from roadside service or towing operator.
3. **Log Operational Delay:** Dispatcher must immediately record a `delay_reported` delivery event in the TMS/Copilot system with estimated downtime.

## 3. Customer & Receiver Escalation Protocol
- **Delay Under 2 Hours:** Update system tracking notes. Monitor roadside service ETA. No immediate customer notification required unless delivery appointment window is within 3 hours.
- **Delay Exceeding 2 Hours:**
  - Send urgent notification to the Account Manager and Customer Support team.
  - Notify receiver of a revised appointment window.
  - Request rescheduling of dock appointment if transit delay causes driver to miss delivery window.

## 4. Cross-Docking & Transloading Procedures
If roadside repairs exceed **6 hours** or equipment is deemed catastrophic:
1. Locate nearest terminal, cross-dock facility, or authorized secondary carrier within a 75-mile radius.
2. Dispatch emergency recovery team or replacement power unit (bobtail).
3. If cargo requires reefer monitoring, ensure auxiliary cooling is operational or transfer cargo immediately to avoid OS&D (Over, Short & Damaged) claims.
